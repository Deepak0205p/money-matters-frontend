# Module 10: TAX BASICS FOR STUDENTS

MODULE 10: TAX BASICS FOR STUDENTS
10.1 Income Tax Kya Hai — Kyun Dena Padta Hai
Income tax woh paisa hai jo aapki aamdani (income) se sarkar kat ti hai. Yeh desh
chalane ke liye chanda hai — sadke, hospital, school, defense sab isse chalte hain.
India mein income tax direct tax  hai — matlab seedha aapki aamdani se cut hota
hai. Har financial year (April 1 to March 31) mein aapki total income calculate hoti
hai, aur uspe tax lagta hai based on "slabs" — jyada income = jyada tax.
Student ke liye important question:  "Kya mujhe tax dena padega?"
Answer:  Agar aapki annual income ₹2.5 lakh se kam hai (old regime) ya ₹4 lakh se
kam hai (new regime), toh aapko ZERO tax  dena hai. Aur new regime mein Section
87A rebate se ₹12 lakh tak ki taxable income pe bhi tax ZERO ho jata hai — salary
₹12.75 lakh tak (₹75,000 standard deduction ke baad taxable ₹12 lakh) = nil tax.
Most students ki income isse kam hoti hai, toh formally tax nahi dena padta. Lekin
ITR file karna still beneficial hai — refund ke liye, loan ke liye, visa ke liye.

Important:  ₹5 lakh tak income pe Section 87A rebate se tax zero ho jata hai (old

tax (FY 2025-26 se). Student  ki  salary  ₹25,000/month  =  ₹3  lakh/year  =
effectively zero tax. Lekin ITR file zaroor karo!
Marginal Relief Note:  Agar taxable income ₹12 lakh se thoda zyada hai (jaise
₹12.1L-₹12.3L zone), toh bhi ghabrane ki baat nahi. Marginal relief se aapka tax
payable ₹12 lakh se upar wali income se zyada nahi hoga. Matlab ₹12.1L pe tax
sirf ₹10,000 tak simit rahega — ₹61,500 ka poora slab tax nahi lagta.
10.2 Important Tax Sections for Students — Detailed with
Examples
Tax savings ke liye government ne kuch sections diye hain jo students ke liye
relevant hain:
Section 80C:
Investment  pe  ₹1.5  lakh  tak  tax  deduction.  ELSS  (SIP),  PPF,  FD  (5-year),  NSC,
insurance premium — sab isme aate hain.
Example: ₹3 lakh taxable income, ₹1.5 lakh ELSS invest = taxable income ₹1.5 lakh
= zero tax! (Old regime mein).
Section 80CCD:
NPS (National Pension Scheme) mein additional ₹50,000 deduction. Agar long-term
retirement planning kar rahe ho, NPS + 80CCD = extra tax save. Total 80C + 80CCD
= ₹2 lakh deduction.
Section 80TTA/80TTB:
Savings account ke interest pe ₹10,000 tak deduction. FD interest pe senior citizens
ke  liye  ₹50,000  (80TTB).  Student  ke  liye  savings  interest  chhota  lekin  useful
deduction.
Example: Savings account mein ₹15,000 interest aaya. 80TTA se ₹10,000 exempt.
Taxable interest = ₹5,000 only.
Section 80GG:
Rent pe rahte ho aur HRA nahi milta? ₹60,000/year tak deduction (conditions apply).
PG/Hostel rent bhi cover hota hai. Form 10BA fill karna padta hai.
Example: ₹5,000/month rent = ₹60,000/year. 80GG se ₹60,000 deduction. Taxable
income kam.
Section 80E:
Education loan ke interest pe deduction — koi limit nahi!  8 saal tak claim kar sakte
hain. Jab EMI start ho, interest portion ka deduction lo.
Example: ₹8 lakh education loan at 9%. First year interest = ₹72,000. 80E se full
₹72,000 deductible. Taxable income directly ₹72,000 kam.
10.3 Form 16 Kya Hai — Kaise Padhein
Form 16 woh document hai jo aapka employer deta hai jo batata hai ki saal bhar
mein aapki kitni salary thi, kitna TDS cut hua, aur kitna tax aapne pay kiya. Yeh ITR
file karne ke liye zaroori hai.
Form 16 mein 2 parts hote hain:
Part A:  Employer  ki  details  aur  TDS  info.  TAN  number,  PAN,  total  TDS
deposited.
Part B:  Salary breakdown, deductions, aur tax calculation. Basic, HRA, LTA,
deductions (80C, 80D), taxable income, tax payable.
Kaise Padhein:
Gross salary check karo — yeh aapki total income.
Exemptions dekho — HRA, LTA, standard deduction (old regime: ₹50,000; new
regime: ₹75,000 FY 2025-26).
Deductions verify karo — 80C, 80D, 80E jo aapne claim kiye.
Taxable income = Gross - Exemptions - Deductions.
Tax calculated = final tax liability.
TDS = tax already paid. Agar TDS > tax liability = refund. Agar TDS < tax =
tax payable.
Job lagne ke baad April-June mein employer se Form 16 mango  — yeh aapka
right hai. Form 16 padhna seekho — usme likha hai ki sarkar ne aapka paisa kahan
lagaya aur kitne deductions claim kiye.
10.4 ITR Filing — Kyun Aur Kaise
ITR (Income Tax Return) woh form hai jo aap sarkar ko apni annual income aur tax ki
detail dene ke liye bharte hain. Agar aapki income taxable limit se kam hai toh bhi
ITR file karna beneficial hai.
4 Reasons to File ITR Even with Zero Tax:
Tax refund claim karne ke liye  — agar TDS cut hua hai aur income
taxable nahi, toh refund milega. ₹2,000 TDS = ₹2,000 refund.
Loan  approval  ke  liye  —  bank  ITR  dekhti  hai.  Home  loan,  car  loan,
personal loan — ITR = income proof.
Visa ke liye  — foreign travel mein ITR proof chahiye. US, UK, Schengen —
sab ITR maangte hain.
Financial record ke liye  — official income history. Future mein business
start karna ho toh ITR useful.
ITR Filing Process — 7 Steps:
Step 1: Go to incometax.gov.in (official website).
Step 2: Register with PAN (already registered hoga agar pehle kiya hai).
Step 3: Select ITR-1 (for salary income) ya ITR-2 (for capital gains).
Step 4: Fill income details from Form
Step 5: Claim deductions under 80C, 80D, 80E etc.
Step 6: Verify tax calculation — payable ya refund.
Step 7: Verify with Aadhaar OTP ya digital signature. Submit!
Free hai — kisi CA ki zaroorat nahi simple salary walo ko.  Time:  15-30
minutes.
Deadline:  July 31 of every year (for previous financial year). Late filing = ₹1,000-
₹5,000 penalty.
ITR-1 vs ITR-2 vs ITR-3:
ITR-1 (Sahaj):  Salary + one house property + other sources (interest). Most
students/freshers.
ITR-2:  Capital gains (SIP, stocks), multiple house property. Agar equity sell
kiya ho.
ITR-3:  Business/profession income. Freelancers, business owners.
ITR-4 (Sugam):  Presumptive income. Freelancers with 44ADA.
Common ITR Mistakes:
Wrong ITR form select karna.
TDS mismatch — Form 16 vs Form 26AS.
Deductions overclaim karna — fake 80C receipts.
Bank interest declare nahi karna — AI se pakde jaoge.
Verify nahi karna — ITR file hua hi nahi!
10.5 Student-Specific Tax Situations
FD Interest Tax:
FD pe jo interest milta hai, uspe TDS 10% cut hota hai (₹40,000/year se zyada hone
pe). Agar total income taxable nahi hai, toh  Form 15G  submit karo — TDS nahi
katega.
Example: ₹50,000 FD interest. Bank ₹5,000 TDS cut kar degi. Form 15G submit kiya
→ TDS zero. Refund bhi nahi chahiye.
SIP Returns Tax:
Equity fund 1 saal se zyada hold pe Long Term Capital Gain (LTCG)  ₹1.25
lakh tak tax-free. Usse zyada pe 12.5% tax.
Debt fund pe aapki slab rate pe tax. Short-term (3 years) = slab rate. Long-
term = 20% with indexation.
Example: ₹2 lakh LTCG equity. ₹1.25 lakh exempt. ₹75,000 pe 12.5% = ₹9,375 tax.
Freelance Income Tax:
Freelance income bhi taxable hai! ₹4L+ pe tax (new regime). 44ADA  ke under 50% presumptive
deduction  available  hai.  Matlab  ₹6  lakh  income  declare  karo,  50%  =  ₹3  lakh
automatic deduction. Taxable = ₹3 lakh. No receipts needed!
Example: Freelance income ₹8 lakh. 44ADA: 50% deduction = ₹4 lakh. Taxable = ₹4
lakh. Tax = ZERO (new regime — ₹4L tak nil slab + 87A rebate).
Stipend Tax:
Stipend taxable hai (salary ke under).
Scholarship tax-free hai (Section 10(16)).
PhD stipend = taxable (if it's employment).
Government scholarship = tax-free.
Verify karo: Stipend = services ke badle? Taxable. Scholarship = merit/need based?
Tax-free.
10.6 TDS Kya Hai — Tax Deducted at Source
TDS (Tax Deducted at Source) ka matlab hai — paisa aane se pehle hi tax cut ho jata
hai. Jaise FD interest ₹50,000 hai, toh bank ₹5,000 TDS cut kar degi, aapko ₹45,000
milega. Yeh advance tax hai — sarkar ko paise baad mein nahi, abhi chahiye.
TDS Check Karne Ke Liye:
Form 26AS:  incometax.gov.in pe — yeh sab TDS details dikhata hai. Har
quarter update hota hai.
AIS (Annual Information Statement):  Naya system — detailed income
breakdown. Banks, employers, mutual funds sab report karte hain.
Agar zyada TDS cut hua hai, toh ITR file karo aur refund lo!  Refund 3-6
months mein bank account mein aata hai.
Common TDS Scenarios:
Salary: Employer TDS cut karta hai (as per Form 16).
FD interest: 10% TDS above ₹40,
Freelance: Client 10% TDS cut karta hai above ₹30,000/transaction.
Rent: T enant 5% TDS above ₹50,000/month.
10.7  Old  Regime  vs  New  Regime  —  Student  Ke  Liye
Kaunsa Better
Comparison:
Factor Old Regime New Regime
Basic exemption ₹2.5L ₹4L
87A rebate (zero tax) Up to ₹5L income Up to ₹12L income
Deductions (80C etc.) Allowed Most NOT allowed
Standard deduction ₹50,000 ₹75,000
Tax rates Higher slabs Lower slabs
Rule of Thumb for Students:
Agar aap 80C mein invest kar rahe ho (SIP , PPF, FD)  → Old Regime
better hai kyunki deductions se taxable income kam hoti hai.
Agar koi investment nahi ho rahi  → New Regime better hai kyunki lower
tax rates hain + higher exemption.
Calculator Example with ₹6 lakh salary:
Old Regime: ₹6,00,000 - ₹50,000 (standard) - ₹1,50,000 (80C ELSS) = ₹4,00,000
taxable. Tax = ₹7,500. 87A rebate = ₹7,500. Tax = ZERO!
New Regime: ₹6,00,000 - ₹75,000 = ₹5,25,000 taxable. Tax = ₹6,250 (₹4L ke
upar 5%). 87A rebate (up to ₹12L) = ₹6,250. Tax = ZERO!
Dono mein zero tax. Lekin agar 80C mein ₹1.5 lakh invest kiya → Old regime mein
habit bani + wealth create. New regime mein no investment = no wealth.
Decision Flowchart:
Kya 80C mein invest kar rahe ho?
    ↓ YES → Old Regime (deductions + lower taxable income)
    ↓ NO → Kya income ₹12L se kam hai?
        ↓ YES → New Regime (lower rates, no deductions needed)
        ↓ NO → Calculate both, jo kam tax de wo lo
Calculator:  incometax.gov.in pe free calculator available. Dono compare karo.

YAHAN PE LOG GALTI KARTE HAIN (Module 10)
ITR file nahi karna kyunki "income kam hai"  — Refund miss ho raha
hai, loan/visa mein problem hogi. File karo!
New regime choose karna bina calculation ke  — Agar 80C deductions
hain toh old regime better. Calculate pehle.
TDS cut hone ke baad refund claim nahi karna  — Form 15G submit
karo ya ITR file karo. Paisa chhod ke mat aao.
Freelance income ko tax-free samajhna  — Yeh fully taxable hai. 44ADA
use karo, lekin declare karo.
Education loan interest ka deduction (80E) claim nahi karna  — Koi
upper limit nahi is deduction ka. Full interest deductible. Miss mat karo.
Form 16 padhna nahi seekhna  — Employer galat TDS cut kar sakta hai.
Verify karo. Form 16 = your right.
July  31  deadline  miss  karna  —  Late  filing  penalty  ₹1,000-₹5,
Calendar mein mark karo.
Tax planning sirf "baad mein" karna  — April se shuru karo. March mein
₹1.5 lakh ek saath nahi daal paoge. SIP se monthly tax planning.
KEY TAKEAWAYS (Module 10)
₹2.5L (old)/₹4L (new) basic exemption, aur new regime mein 87A rebate se ₹12L
tak taxable income (salary ₹12.75L tak) = zero tax  — most students ko
tax nahi dena. Lekin ITR file karo.
ITR file karo even with zero tax  — refund, loan approval, visa ke liye
zaroori. 15 minute online.
Section 80C se ₹1.5L tak tax save  — ELSS best hai students ke liye (3yr
lock-in, 12-15% returns).
TDS check karo Form 26AS mein  — zyada cut hua toh refund lo. Form
15G se TDS roko.
80E se education loan interest ka full deduction lo  — koi limit nahi. 8
saal tak claim karo.
COMMON MISCONCEPTIONS (Module 10)
"Student ko tax se koi lena dena nahi"  — Galat! Freelance income,
stipend, FD interest sab taxable hai. Awareness chahiye.
"ITR filing complicated hai"  — Nahi! ITR-1 online 15 minute mein fill ho
jata hai, free hai. Step-by-step guide available.
"New  regime  hamesha  better  hai"  —  Nahi!  Agar  80C/80D/80E
deductions claim kar rahe ho toh old regime better. Calculate karo.
"Tax planning sirf ameer logo ke liye hai"  — Galat! ₹5,000 ki ELSS se
₹750 tax save ho sakta hai. Har rupee counts.
"Form 16 mil gaya toh kaam khatam"  — Nahi! Form 16 verify karo, ITR
file karo, refund claim karo. Process complete karo.
