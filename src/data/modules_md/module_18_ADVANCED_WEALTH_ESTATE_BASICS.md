### 18.1.1 REITs (Real Estate Investment Trusts) — Commercial Real Estate with ₹500

Imagine karo ek aisa mall ya commercial IT park jahan Google, Microsoft, TCS, Amazon, aur HDFC Bank jaisi giant companies rent deti hain. Us poore commercial complex ki keemat hai ₹5,000 Crore! 

Normal middle-class student ke liye poora tech park khareedna namumkin hai. Lekin **REIT (Real Estate Investment Trust)** is poore tech park ko lakhs of micro-shares (units) mein divide kar deta hai.

#### REIT Ka Working Mechanism:
1. **Asset Pooling:** REIT company (sponsor) Grade-A commercial properties (IT parks, shopping malls, corporate offices) khareedti ya construct karti hai.
2. **Tenant Rent Collection:** Amazon, Google, Accenture jaise corporate clients se har mahine crores ka rent collect hota hai.
3. **SEBI 90% Mandate Rule:** SEBI (Securities and Exchange Board of India) ka strict rule hai ki REITs ko apna **90% Net Distributable Cash Flow (NDCF)** apne unit holders (investors) ko quarterly payout (dividend/interest/repayment) ke roop mein distribute karna HI PADEGA!
4. **Unit Listing:** Stock exchanges (NSE & BSE) par REIT shares ki tarah trade hote hain. Aap Apne Demat Account (Zerodha, Groww, AngelOne) se 1 unit bhi khareed sakte ho!

```
[Commercial Tech Parks / Malls] ──(Rent Payout)──> [REIT Entity]
                                                         │
                                           (SEBI Mandate: 90% NDCF Payout)
                                                         │
                                                         ▼
                                            [Unit Holders / Investors]
                                            (Aap aur Main - Quarterly Income)
```

### 18.1.2 InvITs (Infrastructure Investment Trusts) — India Ki Backbone Mein Investment

Agar REITs commercial real estate (offices, malls) ke landlords hain, toh **InvITs (Infrastructure Investment Trusts)** India ke infrastructure highways, power transmission lines, gas pipelines, aur renewable energy solar parks ke owners hain!

Jab aap NHAI highway par car se jaate ho aur Toll Plaza par Fastag scan hota hai, ya jab aapke ghar mein Power Grid ki transmission line se electricity aati hai — us toll collection aur transmission fee ka ek hissa **InvIT investors** ke pocket mein jaata hai!

#### REITs vs InvITs Comparison:
- **REITs** rely on office rent & retail mall tenant sales.
- **InvITs** rely on government power transmission tariffs, highway toll collection, and long-term infrastructure contracts (often 15 to 30 years concession agreements).

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

#### Yield vs Growth Dynamics in InvITs:
Students ko yeh baat bohot dhyan se samajhni chahiye: **InvITs High Cash Distribution Yield dete hain, par Capital Growth Moderate hoti hai.**

Kyun? Kyunki highway concessions (e.g., 20 saal ka toll contract) time ke saath expire hote hain (asset amortization). InvIT manager ko purane asset ke expire hone se pehle naye highways ya power lines acquire karne padte hain.
- **Ideal For:** Monthly/Quarterly cash flow generation (e.g., college expenses, pocket money, portfolio yield buffering).
- **Core Strategy:** InvIT se aane wale quarterly distributions ko silent mat rehne do — use reinvest karo Nifty 50 Index funds ya Core Equity portfolio mein! (See Section 18.3).

### 18.1.3 Secondary Market SGBs (Sovereign Gold Bonds) — The Hidden Goldmine

Aapne Module 7 mein Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGBs) ke baare mein padha tha:
- RBI dwara issued 8-year tenure bonds.
- 2.5% annual fixed interest payout (paid semi-annually).
- Capital gains tax 100% EXEMPT at maturity (8 years)!

**Lekin 2024-2025 mein ek bada change aaya:** Government of India aur Reserve Bank of India ne fresh SGB issuances ko pause/halt kar diya hai. Government ab high interest cost ki wajah se naye SGB issues kam ya band kar rahi hai.

**Toh kya students ab SGB nahi khareed sakte? WRONG!**
Aap abhi bhi NSE aur BSE ke **Secondary Market** se existing issued SGBs khareed sakte ho — aur woh bhi **Fresh Issue Price se 3% to 8% DISCOUNT Par!**

#### Secondary Market Mein SGB Discount Par Kyun Milte Hain?
1. **Liquidity Need of Original Owners:** Jin logon ne 2019, 2020, ya 2021 mein SGB khareede the, unhe achanak emergency mein cash chahiye. Unka 8-year lock-in poora nahi hua hai.
2. **Low Trading Volume:** Stock market par regular retail buyers ko secondary market SGB symbol search karna nahi aata (e.g., `SGBAUG28`, `SGBDE28`, `SGBMAY29I`). Sellers fast exit ke liye market price se ₹200-₹400 per gram kam price par sell orders daal dete hain!

#### Practical Math Example: Spot Gold vs Secondary SGB

Maan lo aaj 24 Karat Gold ka market spot rate hai **₹7,500 per gram**.

- **Option A: Physical Gold / Digital Gold**
  - Purchase Price: ₹7,500/g + 3% GST (₹225) + Making Charges (₹500) = **₹8,225/g**.
  - Annual Interest: ₹0.
  - Tax at Sale: Slab rate / LTCG tax applicable.

- **Option B: Secondary Market SGB on NSE (Symbol: `SGBMAY29VII`)**
  - NSE Traded Price: **₹7,150 per gram** (Discount of ₹350 from spot price!).
  - GST / Making Charge: ZERO!
  - Annual Interest: 2.5% p.a. on original issue price (credited to bank account every 6 months).
  - Capital Gains Tax at Maturity (May 2029): **ZERO TAX!**
