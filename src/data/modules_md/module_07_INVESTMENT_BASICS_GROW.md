7.1 Investment Kya Hai — Saving Se Kya Different
Saving = paisa protect karna. Investment = paisa grow karna. Yeh basic farq hai. Jab
tum saving account mein paisa rakhte ho, woh 3-4% grow hota hai lekin inflation
5-6% hai — toh real mein tumhara paisa  kam ho raha hai! Investment se tum
inflation ko beat karte ho aur wealth create karte ho. "Paise ko kaam pe lagao,
tum kaam mat karo paise ke liye"  — yeh investing ka core philosophy.

7.2 SIP — Systematic Investment Plan (Complete Guide)
SIP kya hai? Har mahine fixed amount automatically invest karo — ₹100 se start
ho sakti hai!  Yeh bahut log nahi jaante. SIP ka magic  "rupee cost averaging"
mein hai — market up ho ya down, tum har mahine same amount invest karte ho.
Jab market down hai, zyada units milte hain (sasta!). Jab market up hai, kam units
milte hain (mehenga!). Average cost balanced rehta hai.
Rupee Cost Averaging Example:
Month NAV Invested Units Bought
1 ₹20 ₹1,000 50.0
2 ₹15 ₹1,000 66.7
3 ₹25 ₹1,000 40.0
4 ₹20 ₹1,000 50.0
Total — ₹4,000 206.7
Average NAV = ₹20. But average cost = ₹4,000 / 206.7 = ₹19.35. Market fluctuation
ke baad bhi tumhara cost ₹20 se kam! Yeh SIP ka power.
Step-by-Step: SIP Kaise Shuru Karein:
Step 1: KYC complete karo (PAN + Aadhaar + selfie). Online 10 minute.
Step 2: App download karo (Groww / Zerodha Kite / Paytm Money / ET Money —
teeno reliable, SEBI registered).
Step 3: Account verify karo — 2-3 minute mein online.
Step 4: Fund choose karo — beginners ke liye Nifty 50 Index Fund  best hai.
Step 5: Amount set karo — ₹100 minimum, ₹500-₹1,000 recommended.
Step 6: Auto-debit ON karo — date fix karo (salary ke 2-3 din baad). Done!
NAV Kya Hai:  Net Asset Value — ek mutual fund unit ki price. Jaise share ka price,
waise mutual fund unit ka NAV. ₹20 NAV = ₹20 mein 1 unit milta hai. NAV daily
change hota hai.
Units Kya Hai:  Mutual fund mein "shares" nahi hote, "units" hote hain. ₹1,000
invest kiya, NAV ₹20 = 50 units mile. NAV ₹25 hua = tumhara 50 units × ₹25 =
₹1,250 value.
Entry/Exit Load:  Most funds mein ab koi load nahi hai (SEBI ne band kar diya).
Lekin check karo — kuch funds mein 1% exit load 1 saal ke andar redemption pe.

SIP Ke 7 Benefits:
Disciplined investing — auto-debit, no emotional decisions.
Rupee cost averaging — market up/down doesn't matter.
Power of compounding — long-term mein exponential growth.
Flexibility — amount change karo, pause karo, stop karo — anytime.
Low minimum — ₹100 se start. Koi excuse nahi.
No timing needed — "best time to invest was yesterday, second best is
today."
Tax efficient — long-term capital gains ₹1.25 lakh tak tax-free (equity).
SIP Ke 5 Myths Busted:
"SIP mein loss ho sakta hai"  — Short-term mein yes, long-term (7+
years) mein historically Nifty 50 positive raha hai.
"Market crash mein SIP band karo"  — Yeh EXACTLY woh time hai jab
zyada units milte hain. Continue karo!
"₹100 se kya hoga"  — ₹100/m at 12% for 30 years = ₹3.5 lakh+. Hota
hai.
"SIP lock-in hota hai"  — Nahi! ELSS mein 3-year lock-in hai, baaki SIP
mein koi lock-in nahi. Anytime redeem.
"SIP stocks se kam return deti hai"  — Individual stocks risky hain. SIP
index fund = average market return, safer.

Direct vs Regular Plan:
Direct plan mein expense ratio kam hoti hai (0.5-1% less), toh returns zyada milte
hain. Long-term mein yeh farq bahut bada hota hai. Always choose DIRECT plan.
Example: ₹5,000/month SIP for 20 years.
- Regular plan (1.5% expense): Corpus = ₹43 lakh
- Direct plan (0.5% expense): Corpus = ₹50 lakh
- Farq = ₹7 lakh! Sirf expense ratio ka.
Expense Ratio:  Fund manage karne ka fee. Kam hone chahiye. Index fund mein
0.1-0.3% hoti hai (lowest), active fund mein 1-2% (higher).
NAV:  Net Asset Value — ek unit ki price. Daily change hota hai.
AUM:  Assets Under Management — fund mein total kitna paisa hai. Zyada AUM =
stable fund, lekin very high AUM = difficult to manage (especially mid/small cap).
Rating:  CRISIL, Morningstar ratings dekho. 5-star = historically good, lekin past
performance guarantee nahi.

7.5 PPF — Public Provident Fund
PPF government scheme hai — 15 year lock-in, tax-free return, currently  7.1%
interest (Q1 FY 2025-26, unchanged for 5+ years). Minimum ₹500/year, maximum
₹1.5  lakh/year.  EEE  status  =  Exempt-Exempt-Exempt  —  investment  pe  tax
deduction, interest pe tax-free, maturity pe tax-free. Kahan kholein: Bank ya Post
Office.

Premature Withdrawal Rules:
After 5 years, specific conditions pe partial withdrawal allowed. Maximum 50% of
balance. Education, medical emergency ke liye.
Loan Against PPF:
3rd to 6th year between loan allowed. 25% of balance. Interest 1% above PPF rate.
Repayment 36 months mein.
Student ke liye PPF:  Long-term tax-saving tool. 18 saal ki umr mein shuru karo,
33 pe maturity = ₹16 lakh+ (₹5,000/month). Agar 25 pe start kiya toh 40 pe =
same amount. Early start = magic.
7.6 Stock Market Basics
Stock market kya hai? Yeh ek marketplace hai jahan companies ke shares khareede-
bike jaate hain. Share = company ka chhota ownership piece. Agar company achi
karti  hai,  share  price  badhta  hai  —  tum  profit  kamao.  BSE  (Bombay  Stock
Exchange)  aur NSE (National Stock Exchange)  — India ke 2 main exchanges.
Sensex  = BSE ke top 30 companies ka index. Nifty 50  = NSE ke top 50 companies
ka index.
Demat Account:
Shares digital form mein hold karne ka account. Kaise kholein: Zerodha, Groww,
Upstox — online 10 minute. Charges: Account opening free (usually), AMC ₹300/
year, brokerage 0.03% or flat ₹
Beginner Ke Liye Safe Ya Nahi — Honest Answer:
Direct stock picking risky hai beginner ke liye. Better option: Index fund  — Nifty 50
index fund mein invest karo, market ka average return milega. Individual stock pick
karna research aur experience chahta hai. 90% retail investors market ko beat nahi
kar paate.
Index Fund Better Hai Individual Stocks Se — 5 Reasons:
Diversification — 50 companies ek saath. Ek company fail = no problem.
Professional management — fund manager track karta hai.
Low cost — expense ratio 0.1% vs brokerage + research cost.
No emotional decisions — SIP auto chalta hai.
Historically consistent — Nifty 50 long-term ~12%.
10 Golden Rules Agar Stock Market Mein Jaana Hai:
Pehle emergency fund banao.
Pehle SIP start karo index fund mein.
Direct stocks ke liye alag capital rakho — "fun money" jo 100% loss afford
kar sako.
Kabhi borrowed money se stock mat kharido.
1 stock pe >10% capital mat lagao.
Long-term invest karo — trading nahi.
F&O (Futures & Options) bilkul mat karo beginner mein — 90% loss hota hai
retail investors ko.
Research bina investment mat karo — annual report, balance sheet padho.
Loss afford kar sako toh hi invest karo.
"Hot tip" pe paisa mat lagao — WhatsApp forward ≠ research.
Fundamental Analysis Basics (Simple Introduction):
PE  Ratio:  Price  to  Earnings.  Kitne  saal  mein  company  profits  se  price
recover hoga. 15 = reasonable, 30 = expensive, <10 = cheap (maybe).
Market Cap:  Company ka total value. Large cap = safe, small cap = risky.
Debt:  Company pe kitna loan hai. Kam debt = healthy.
ROE:  Return on Equity. 15%+ = good management.
Detailed stock analysis Module 8 mein nahi, advanced course lo ya advisor consult
karo.

7.8 Crypto — One Honest Section
Crypto kya hai? Digital currency jo blockchain technology pe based hai. Bitcoin,
Ethereum — sabse popular. Lekin student ke liye 5 strict warnings:
80% crypto projects fail  — long-term survival uncertain. 2017 ke 100 top
coins mein se 80 ab zero hain.
India mein koi regulation nahi  — agar fraud hua toh koi protect nahi
karega. Exchange band ho gayi = paisa gayab.
30% tax on gains + 1% TDS  — returns ka almost third tax mein jaata hai.
Plus 4% cess.
Extreme volatility  — ek hafte mein 50% up ya 50% down possible. ₹1 lakh
→ ₹50,000 in 3 days.
Scams  bahut  common  —  fraud  exchanges,  rug  pulls,  fake  coins.
"Guaranteed 10x" = 100% scam.
Recommendation for Students:  Pehle basics strong karo — FD, SIP, PPF . Crypto
LATER, agar kabhi, aur sirf utna jo 100% loss afford kar sako . Crypto ≠ investing,
crypto = speculation. Difference samjho.

7.10 Tax Basics for Students (Cross-reference Module 10)
Quick overview:
- Income tax slab: 0-₹3L = no tax (new regime). Most students isse kam hain.
- Section 80C: ₹1.5L tak deduction — ELSS, PPF, 5yr FD.
- Section 80CCD: NPS mein extra ₹50K deduction.
- Section 80TTA: Savings interest ₹10K tak tax-free.
- Section 80E: Education loan interest — full deduction, no limit, 8 years.
- Form 16: Employer deta hai — salary + TDS summary.
- ITR: File karo even if zero tax — refund, loan, visa ke liye proof.
