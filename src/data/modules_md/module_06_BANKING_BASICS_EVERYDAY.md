₹5,000/month FD Laddering Strategy:
Har mahine ₹5,000 ka 1-year FD. 12 months mein 12 FDs. Har mahine ek FD
mature = ₹5,000 + interest liquid. Best for regular income.
Tax-Saving FD ₹1,50,000 (5 years):
Principal  =  ₹1,50,000.  Interest  6.5%  =  ₹9,750/year.  5  years  =  ₹48,750
interest. Maturity = ₹1,98,750. Tax benefit 80C = ₹46,800 save (30% slab
pe). Effective return = much higher.
Premature Withdrawal:
Penalty 0.5-1% interest rate cut. Example: 6.5% FD pe 1% penalty = 5.5% actual.
Agar 6 months mein toda 5-year FD ko, toh loss hoga. Emergency mein hi todna
chahiye.
Tax on FD Interest:
Section 80TTA: Savings account interest ₹10,000 tak tax-free. FD interest pe
nahi lagta.
TDS: FD interest pe 10% TDS cut hota hai agar interest ₹40,000+/year
(₹50,000+ for seniors).
Form 15G: Agar total income taxable nahi hai, toh Form 15G submit karo —
TDS nahi katega.
6.3 Recurring Deposit (RD) — Monthly Saving Habit
RD mein har mahine fixed amount deposit karte ho, maturity pe lump sum milta hai.
Best for students kyunki chhota amount monthly deposit kar sakte ho. ₹100 se start
ho sakta hai!
How RD Works:
Step 1: Decide amount (₹500/month) and tenure (5 years).
Step 2: Auto-debit setup — har mahine fixed date pe amount cut.
Step 3: Maturity pe lump sum + interest milta hai.

6.4 Digital Payments — UPI, NEFT, IMPS, RTGS
UPI (Unified Payments Interface):
Kya hai: NPCI ka system jo bank accounts ko link karta hai for instant transfer. Kyun
free hai: Government ne promote kiya hai digital India ke liye. Daily limit: Usually ₹1
lakh/day (some banks ₹2 lakh). Best for: Daily payments, P2P transfer, merchant
payment.
NEFT (National Electronic Funds Transfer):
Speed: 30 min - 2 hours. Limit: No limit. Charges: Free (most banks). Availability:
24/7. Best for: Large transfers, non-urgent.
IMPS (Immediate Payment Service):
Speed: Instant. Limit: ₹5 lakh. Charges: ₹5-15. Availability: 24/7. Best for: Urgent
transfer, any time.
RTGS (Real Time Gross Settlement):
Speed: Instant. Limit: ₹2 lakh minimum. Charges: ₹25-50. Availability: 24/7 (now).
Best for: Very large transfers.

6.7 KYC and Online Safety — 15 Safety Tips
KYC = Know Your Customer:
Yeh verification process hai jisme bank tumhari identity verify karta hai. Documents:
Aadhaar + PAN + Photo. Aadhaar e-KYC se online 2 minute mein ho jaata hai. Video
KYC bhi available hai kuch banks mein — ghar baith ke account khul jaata hai.
Online Banking ke 15 Safety Tips:
OTP kisi ko bhi share mat karo  — bank employee bhi nahi maangta. OTP
= password. Share kiya = account khali.
Phishing links pe click mat karo  — email/SMS mein fake bank links. URL
check karo — sbi.co.in vs sbi-bank.co.in (fake).
Public WiFi pe banking mat karo  — data steal ho sakta hai. Man-in-the-
middle attack possible. Mobile data use karo.
Password strong rakho  — date of birth nahi. Mix of uppercase, lowercase,
numbers, symbols. 12+ characters.
2-factor authentication on karo  — SMS OTP + app notification. Double
security.
Screen lock lagao phone pe  — PIN, pattern, fingerprint. Phone chori ho
toh banking app safe.
Banking app logout karo use ke baad  — Auto-lock 2 minutes. Don't stay
logged in.
Unknown numbers se call pe bank details mat do  — Bank kabhi call pe
OTP/CVV nahi maangta. Call wala = scammer.
UPI PIN change regularly karo  — 3 months mein ek baar. 1234, 0000,
birth date mat rakhna.
SIM swap fraud se bacho  — duplicate SIM ke liye alert raho. Agar network
suddenly gayab ho = call customer care immediately.
Loan app se bacho  — 90% fraud hain. Chinese loan apps, harassment,
fake interest. Government registered banks se hi loan lo.
"Lottery/prize" SMS ignore karo  — 100% scam. "You won ₹5 lakh" =
trap. Koi free mein ₹5 lakh nahi deta.
QR code scan mat karo unknown source se  — QR scan = paying, not
receiving. "Scan to receive money" = scam.
Instagram/WhatsApp  pe  investment  tip  pe  paisa  mat  lagao  —
"Guaranteed  20%  return"  =  100%  scam.  SEBI  registered  advisor  se  hi
consult karo.
Bank ka official number/customer care hi use karo  — Card pe likha
number ya official website se lo. Google pe "SBI customer care" mat search
karo — fake numbers aate hain.

YAHAN PE LOG GALTI KARTE HAIN (Module 6)
Zero balance account nahi kholna  — Minimum balance penalty monthly
kat ti hai. ₹100-₹600/quarter = ₹400-₹2,400/year waste.
Public WiFi pe banking karna  — Data theft ka high risk. Cafes, stations,
malls — kabhi banking mat karo.

OTP share karna  — Kisi ko bhi nahi, bank staff bhi nahi maangta. OTP =
account ka key.
FD ka premature withdrawal repeatedly karna  —  Penalty  +  lower
return. Emergency fund alag rakho taak FD na todna pade.
Multiple UPI apps rakhna  — Confusion + security risk dono badhte hain.
Ek app, ek PIN, simple.
Debit card details unknown websites pe save karna  — "Remember
card" mat karo. Data breach mein leak hoga.
E-statement nahi check karna  — Hidden charges pata nahi chalengi.
Monthly 5 minute check karo.
Bank account dormant chhodna  — Inactivity charges + account freeze.
Har 3 mahine mein ek transaction karo.
KEY TAKEAWAYS (Module 6)
Student zero-balance savings account best hai  — no minimum balance
tension. SBI, Kotak, HDFC sab offer karte hain.
FD aur RD safe options hain  — guaranteed return with zero market risk.
RD ₹100 se start. FD ₹1,000 se.
UPI daily payments ke liye best  — instant, free, 24/7. Limit ₹1 lakh/day.
Hidden charges se bachne ke liye e-statements check karo monthly
— ₹2,000-₹5,000/year save possible.
OTP kisi ko share nahi karna  — yeh golden rule hai banking safety ka.
90% frauds OTP se shuru hote hain.
COMMON MISCONCEPTIONS (Module 6)
"Bank sab safe hai"  — Galat! Online frauds common hain, awareness
zaroori hai. Bank account safe hai, lekin tumhara phone unsafe ho sakta hai.
"FD se zyada return kahin nahi"  — Galat! SIP historically 12-15% return
diya hai long-term mein. FD 6.5% = inflation barely beat karta hai.
"Debit card aur credit card ek jaisa hai"  — Nahi! Debit = apna paisa,
Credit = udhaar. Credit card = bank ka paisa, 36-48% interest.
"Online banking risky hai"  — Safe hai agar rules follow karo — OTP mat
share karo, phishing se bacho, strong password rakho.
"KYC ek baar ki formality hai"  — Nahi! Periodic KYC update zaroori hai
account active rakhne ke liye. Every 2-3 years update karo.
